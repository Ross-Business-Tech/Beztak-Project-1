Beztakets privacy policy

Beztakets is a class demo project. It is not a commercial service.

The app connects to one test Gmail account to read emails sent to it and to send replies. It uses that access only to create help-desk tickets from those emails and to send replies written by the team.

We do not sell or share email content, and we do not use it for advertising. Test emails are stored only for as long as the demo runs. Please send only made-up test messages, not personal information.

Questions: contact the project team through the test Gmail address.
