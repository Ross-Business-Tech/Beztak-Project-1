What the briefing covers

  1. Where we stand. The share of questions the bot answered, against your goal.
  2. The trend. Six months, with the manual updates marked.
  3. Why people reach a person. What the manual can fix, and what is by design.
  4. What to change. The three best changes, with the extra questions each would answer.
  5. Return on updates. Questions answered, fewer tickets, and staff hours saved.
  6. Decisions. Three decisions to close the meeting, the next target, and your note.

Plus a hidden appendix: the top questions for each category that needs work, and a working table to decide, assign and track each change.
