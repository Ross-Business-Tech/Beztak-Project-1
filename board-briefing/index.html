What you get

  1. Executive deck (6 slides). For leadership: did we hit the goal, the trend, what people ask most, what past updates achieved, and what comes next.
  2. Manual Improvement Dashboard (2 pages). For the team: every change to make, ranked. Open one to see all the questions behind it, then mark it an action item or dismiss it, and assign an owner.

The answer rate leaves out questions that should go to a person (personal or approval questions, and topics outside the manual), and says how many.
