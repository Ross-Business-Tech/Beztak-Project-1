MONTHLY BOARD BRIEFING
======================

Turns a month of chatbot questions into two slide decks, ready to attach to an email.
Nothing is installed and nothing leaves your computer.

  EXECUTIVE UPDATE (5 slides)   The one your client presents to their boss. How things
                                are going: what people ask most, did we hit the goal,
                                what we changed before and what it achieved.
  IMPROVEMENT PLAN (6 slides)   The condensed working version. The same numbers in
                                brief, then what to fix next, an action table to assign
                                owners, and the next steps.


THE MONTHLY ROUTINE  (about five minutes)
-----------------------------------------
1. Double-click  index.html  in this folder. It opens in your web browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox). No internet connection is needed.
2. Click "Choose a file" and pick this month's questions export (CSV or JSON).
   The file should hold several months of history, so the trend can be drawn.
   The latest complete month is chosen for you.
3. Add your name, your goal, and a short note if you like. These are remembered for next month.
4. Choose a deck at the top of the preview (Executive update or Improvement plan), look it over,
   then pick how to send it:
     Download PowerPoint ................ the deck, to attach to any email
     Download both decks ................ both PowerPoints at once
     Email draft with the deck attached . a file that opens in Outlook or Apple Mail
                                          with the addresses filled in and the deck already
                                          attached. Review, then send.
     Save as PDF ........................ choose "Save as PDF" in the print window
     Copy email text .................... the subject and message, to paste anywhere
   Each deck has its own email text, so the boss gets the update and the team gets the plan.

Want to see it first? Click "Try it with sample data" (made-up questions).


WHAT THE SLIDES SAY
-------------------
Every content slide has a numbered tab, a headline that states the takeaway, and a dark line
at the bottom: a TAKEAWAY in the update, a NEXT STEP in the plan.

EXECUTIVE UPDATE
1. Cover          One sentence that sums up the month.
2. Did we hit the goal   The share of questions the bot answered, against the goal.
3. The trend      Six months of answer rate, with manual updates marked.
4. Top questions  The six topics people asked about most, and how many the bot answered.
5. What we changed  Each manual update, the questions it answered, tickets avoided, hours saved.

IMPROVEMENT PLAN
1. Cover
2. Snapshot       Goal progress, the tickets the manual could have answered, hours saved so far.
3. Why tickets    How many questions went to a person, and how many the manual could have answered.
4. What to change The three best changes, and how many more questions each would answer.
5. Action plan    A table: tick Action item or Dismiss, name an owner and a date, track status.
6. Next steps     Three things to agree, the next target, and your note.

Each slide has speaker notes that explain how the numbers were worked out.
The PowerPoint is fully editable if you want to change a word or a color.

THE HIDDEN APPENDIX  (in the Improvement plan, after the last slide)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Hidden slides stay out of the slide show but travel inside the PowerPoint file.
  One slide per category that needs work: the top questions people asked, why each went to a
  person, how staff resolved it, and how many more questions fixing it would answer.
  If there are more than six suggested changes, the rest of the action table is here too.
To show a hidden slide while presenting, right-click it in PowerPoint and choose Hide Slide
again. "Save as PDF" leaves the appendix out unless you tick the box beside the button.
The action table is a normal PowerPoint table: type into it and add rows. A change you
dismissed will show up again next month if the questions keep coming.


THE QUESTIONS FILE
------------------
See sample/sample-questions.csv for the exact layout. One row per question.

Required columns:
  question_id         a unique ID
  question_text       what the employee asked
  submitted_at        date and time asked, e.g. 2026-10-14T09:30:00Z
  resolution_status   Answered, Ticket Open or Ticket Closed

Strongly recommended (the briefing is much richer with them):
  escalation_reason   why it went to a person: no-match, unclear, policy or out-of-scope
  theme               the topic the bot grouped the question under
  manual_section      the manual section the bot used or cited
  section_published_at  when that section was added to the manual (this is how
                      "return on updates" is measured)
  resolution_note     how the person resolved the ticket (becomes a starting point
                      for new wording)
  category            Safety & Compliance, Equipment & Maintenance, Quality & Audits,
                      Shifts & Staffing, Procurement & Suppliers or Reporting & Records

Rows that cannot be read are skipped, and the page tells you how many and why.


HOW THE NUMBERS ARE WORKED OUT
------------------------------
Answer rate ........ questions answered from the manual / all questions asked that month.
Fixable tickets .... tickets where the manual had nothing, or the section did not settle it.
                     Tickets that go to a person by policy, or are outside the manual, are
                     not counted as fixable.
"More answered" .... each suggested change is assumed to let the bot answer the same share
                     of repeat questions that your past updates did (a typical 80% if there
                     is no history). It is an estimate, not a promise.
Staff hours saved .. questions answered x minutes a person would have spent on a ticket
                     (default 12, change it in step 3) / 60.


PRIVACY
-------
The file you choose is read inside your browser and never uploaded. The page remembers only
your settings (name, goal, note, addresses) on this computer. Avoid putting names or other
personal details of employees into the questions file.
