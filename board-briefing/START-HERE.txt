MONTHLY BOARD BRIEFING
======================

One questions file, two things:

  EXECUTIVE DECK (6 slides)             For leadership. Did we hit the goal, the trend, what people
                                        ask most, what past manual updates achieved, and what
                                        comes next. Download as PowerPoint or PDF, or open an
                                        email draft with the deck attached.
  MANUAL IMPROVEMENT DASHBOARD (2 pages) For the team. Every change to make to the manual, ranked.
                                        Open one to see every question behind it, then mark it an
                                        action item, in progress, done or dismissed, and assign an
                                        owner.

Nothing is installed and nothing leaves your computer.


THE MONTHLY ROUTINE  (about five minutes)
-----------------------------------------
1. Double-click  index.html  in this folder. It opens in your web browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox). No internet connection is needed.
2. Under Setup, click "Choose a file" and pick this month's questions export (CSV or JSON).
   It should hold several months of history so the trend can be drawn. The latest complete
   month is chosen for you. Add your goal, your name, your team members' names and a short
   note if you like. These are remembered for next month.
3. Executive deck tab: look the slides over, then pick how to send them.
     Download PowerPoint ................ the deck, to attach to any email
     Email draft with the deck attached . a file that opens in Outlook or Apple Mail with the
                                          addresses filled in and the deck already attached
     Save as PDF ........................ choose "Save as PDF" in the print window
     Copy email text .................... the subject and message, to paste anywhere
4. Manual improvement dashboard tab: work through the changes with your team (see below).

The page keeps the last questions file on this computer, so the dashboard is there the next
time you open it. "Forget the saved file" under Setup removes it. Want to see everything first?
Click "Try it with sample data" (made-up questions).


THE ANSWER RATE
---------------
The rate on every slide is the fair one:

     answered by the bot  /  (questions asked  -  questions that go to a person on purpose)

Questions that go to a person on purpose are personal, confidential or approval questions, and
topics outside operating procedures (parking, IT). The bot is not meant to answer them, so they
are left out of the rate. The deck always says how many were left out and shows the sum.


THE EXECUTIVE DECK
------------------
Every content slide has a numbered tab, a headline that states the takeaway, and a dark
TAKEAWAY line at the bottom that the presenter can read out. Speaker notes explain how each
number was worked out. The PowerPoint is fully editable.

1. Cover
2. Did we hit the goal   The rate against the goal, and how the questions split: answered,
                         not answered, and the ones that go to a person on purpose.
3. The trend             The rate by month, with the manual updates marked and how many
                         questions were asked each month.
4. Top questions         The six topics people asked about most, over the last three months.
5. What we changed       Each past manual update: questions answered, tickets avoided, staff
                         hours saved (an estimate, and the minutes per ticket are shown).
6. What comes next       The three biggest gaps in the manual and the answers each would add.


THE MANUAL IMPROVEMENT DASHBOARD
--------------------------------
Page 1, the list. Every change to make, ranked by tickets over the last three months so that
one odd month does not reorder the list. Each row shows the type (add a section, or improve
one), the category, the tickets, the answers it would add per month, its status and its owner.
Filter by status or category, or search. "Open" hides anything done or dismissed.

Page 2, one change. Click a row to see:
  - the numbers: tickets, how often asked, the answers it would add, the staff time
  - every question behind it, not answered first, with the date, why it went to a person,
    the ticket, and how a person resolved it (search, filter and page through them)
  - a starting point for the wording: how staff resolved the tickets, with a Copy button
  - your decision: Action item, In progress, Done or Dismissed; an owner (type a name, or
    click a team member); a due date; and notes. It saves as you type.

Decisions are remembered from month to month. A dismissed change stays dismissed. To share
the work with colleagues: "Download spreadsheet" gives an Excel-friendly file, and "Save team
file" / "Load team file" moves everyone's decisions between computers (the newer decision wins).


THE QUESTIONS FILE
------------------
See sample/sample-questions.csv for the exact layout. One row per question.

Required columns:
  question_id         a unique ID
  question_text       what the employee asked
  submitted_at        date and time asked, e.g. 2026-10-14T09:30:00Z
  resolution_status   Answered, Ticket Open or Ticket Closed

Strongly recommended (without them the page warns you, and the numbers are less useful):
  escalation_reason   why it went to a person: no-match, unclear, policy or out-of-scope
                      (without it every question sent to a person counts as a gap)
  theme               the topic the bot grouped the question under
                      (without it, questions are grouped by their exact wording)
  manual_section      the manual section the bot used or cited
  section_published_at  when that section was added to the manual (this is how
                      "what we changed" is measured)
  resolution_note     how the person resolved the ticket (the dashboard's wording starting point)
  category            Safety & Compliance, Equipment & Maintenance, Quality & Audits,
                      Shifts & Staffing, Procurement & Suppliers or Reporting & Records

Rows that cannot be read are skipped, and the page tells you how many and why.


HOW THE OTHER NUMBERS ARE WORKED OUT
------------------------------------
"More answered" .... each change is assumed to let the bot answer the same share of repeat
                     questions that your past updates did (a typical 80% if there is no
                     history). It is an estimate, not a promise.
Staff hours saved .. questions answered x minutes a person would have spent on a ticket
                     (default 12, change it in Setup) / 60. It assumes each answered question
                     would otherwise have become a ticket.


PRIVACY
-------
The file you choose is read inside your browser and never uploaded. The page keeps your
settings, your decisions and the last file on this computer only. Avoid putting names or other
personal details of employees into the questions file.
